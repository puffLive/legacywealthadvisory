# AI search editorial and measurement checklist

For each new article: answer the question directly, explain assumptions, cite primary Canadian sources, include a practical example where appropriate, link relevant calculators and service pages, and check time-sensitive facts before publishing. Record genuine publication and modification dates. Do not claim personal authorship or review unless it occurred.

Measurement: verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools ownership and submit sitemap.xml. In an authorized GA4 property, create a referral report for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, copilot.microsoft.com, gemini.google.com and claude.ai, including source/medium and landing page. Track consultation clicks and confirmed bookings separately. Do not transmit calculator inputs, financial details or contact information to analytics. Review consent and privacy requirements before enabling tracking. Referral attribution is incomplete and does not measure all AI mentions.

Weekly: record date, platform, prompt, citations, linked pages and brand mentions for a consistent set of local and Canadian financial questions. No ranking guarantees.
